# Third-party components

The widget uses the locally installed Consolas font by default. Font files are not bundled in the package.

The twelve SVGs in `resources/` are the previously supplied widget artwork, copied from `Widget/v2-wireviewasus/resources`. They are retained unchanged. Existing artwork rights and licenses continue to apply; no new rights or Marketplace redistribution permission is claimed.

MediaViewer behavior and hexToRGB are adapted from the CORSAIR SDK references supplied in this workspace. No external scripts, fonts or iCUE sensor/media providers are loaded at runtime. Development dependencies remain in source tooling and are not shipped inside the package.
